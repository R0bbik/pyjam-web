Music tooling

Publish, schedule and measure. In one place.

PyJam is the private toolkit behind the independent Nordic dark-folk project Draugskald. It takes our own videos to every platform on time and shows how they perform.

engine

PyJamCore

Reads statistics of Draugskald's own channels on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, Threads, TikTok and streaming services into a private database.

dashboard

PyJamOS

Turns those numbers into a clear overview: watch time, growth and what each release does.

upload

PyJam Warp

Uploads and schedules our videos, Reels and posts to YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, Threads and TikTok, with thumbnails, playlists and first comments.

PyJam is operated by the owner of the Draugskald accounts for those accounts only. It is not a public service and does not let other people sign in. Read the Privacy Policy, Terms of Service and how to delete data.
