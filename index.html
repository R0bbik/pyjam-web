PyJam

Private tooling for the independent music project Draugskald.

PyJam is a small set of tools that the Draugskald project uses to publish its own music videos and to understand how its own channels perform. It is operated by the owner of the Draugskald accounts for those accounts only; it is not offered to other users.

Components

  • PyJamCore reads performance statistics of the Draugskald channels (YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, Threads, TikTok, streaming services) and stores them in the project's private database.
  • PyJamOS shows these statistics to the project owner.
  • PyJam Warp uploads and schedules Draugskald's own videos and posts to YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, Threads and TikTok, adds playlists, thumbnails and the first comment.
