Data Deletion

How to remove data PyJam holds.

PyJam only processes data of the Draugskald project's own accounts. To have any data deleted, or if you believe PyJam holds data about you (for example a public comment you wrote), email honzarobotrobotka@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion”. The request is handled within 30 days and you receive a confirmation.

You can also remove PyJam's access yourself: Facebook and Instagram in Settings → Business integrations, Google in Google security settings, TikTok in Settings → Security → Manage app permissions. When access is removed, the related tokens stop working and stored data is deleted.
